# Reference: https://legislation.gov.au/Details/C2004A01321
# Section: Section 5 - Interpretation
# Original: veteran means a person who has served in the naval, military or air forces of the Commonwealth and has been discharged from those forces
the person is a veteran
    AND the person has served in the naval military or air forces
    AND the person has been discharged from those forces

# Reference: https://legislation.gov.au/Details/C2004A01321
# Section: Section 5A - Qualifying service
# Original: A person has qualifying service if the person has rendered warlike service or non-warlike service
the person has qualifying service
    OR the person has rendered warlike service
    OR the person has rendered non-warlike service

# Reference: https://legislation.gov.au/Details/C2004A01321
# Section: Section 5B - Operational service
# Original: A person has operational service if the person has rendered continuous full-time service on or after 22 May 1986
the person has operational service
    AND the person has rendered continuous full-time service
    AND enlistment date >= operational service start date

# Reference: https://www.dva.gov.au/operational-policy/service-types
# Section: DVA Operational Policy - Service Classifications
# Original: DVA policy defines service types for eligibility assessment based on operational context
the service type matches DVA classification
    AND service type IS IN LIST: DVA operational service type

# Reference: https://www.dva.gov.au/operational-policy/service-types
# Section: DVA Operational Policy - Qualifying Service Thresholds
# Original: DVA policy requires a minimum period of continuous full-time service for certain operational classifications
the service meets DVA qualifying period
    AND period of service in days >= DVA policy continuous full time service threshold
    AND service type IS IN LIST: DVA qualifying service type

# Reference: https://legislation.gov.au/Details/C2004A01321
# Section: Section 5A(2) - Qualifying service periods
# Original: For the purposes of this Act, service is qualifying if rendered during specified operational periods or under specific declarations
the person has qualifying service under DVA policy
    AND qualifying service policy pathway virtual ONE
        OR DVA policy qualifying service path
            AND service type IS IN LIST: DVA qualifying service type
            AND enlistment date >= qualifying service start date
        OR operational service path
            AND the person has operational service

# Reference: https://www.dva.gov.au/operational-policy/service-history-assessment
# Section: DVA Operational Policy - Service History Evaluation
# Original: Each period of service in a veteran's history must be evaluated against operational and qualifying criteria
the service history meets DVA criteria
    AND ALL period IN service history
        AND period evaluation virtual ONE
            OR operational period path
                AND period.service type IS IN LIST: DVA operational service type
            OR qualifying period path
                AND period.service type IS IN LIST: DVA qualifying service type
                AND period.period of service in days >= DVA policy minimum operational period

# Reference: https://legislation.gov.au/Details/C2004A01321
# Section: Section 5B(1A) - Modern operational service
# Original: A person has operational service if the person has rendered continuous full-time service on or after 1 July 1994 in an operational area
the person has modern operational service
    AND the person has rendered continuous full-time service
    AND enlistment date >= modern operational service date
    AND service type IS IN LIST: DVA operational service type

# Reference: https://www.dva.gov.au/operational-policy/service-history-assessment
# Section: DVA Operational Policy - Exclusion of Non-Operational Service
# Original: Service that does not meet the operational or qualifying thresholds is excluded from pension eligibility calculations
the service does not qualify
    AND exclusion pathway virtual ONE
        OR DVA policy non-qualifying exclusion
            AND NOT service type IS IN LIST: DVA operational service type
        OR DVA policy period exclusion
            AND period of service in days < DVA policy minimum operational period
