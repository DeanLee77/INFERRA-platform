# Section: Section 14 - Compensation for injuries
# Original: Where an employee suffers an injury resulting in death, incapacity for work, or permanent impairment, Comcare (or the MRCC for defence members) is liable to pay compensation in accordance with this Part.
DRCA liability to pay compensation established
    AND DRCA part I sections 1 to 13A convergence met
    AND claim for compensation lodged
    AND MANDATORY commission determination required

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 15 - Compensation for property loss or damage
# Original: Compensation is payable for the loss or damage to property (e.g., clothing, medical aids, or vehicles) that occurred in the course of the employee's employment or as a result of an accepted injury.
DRCA property loss or damage compensation met
    AND DRCA liability to pay compensation established
    AND property damaged in course of employment

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 16 - Compensation in respect of medical treatment
# Original: Where an employee suffers an accepted injury, the relevant authority is liable to pay compensation in respect of the cost of medical treatment obtained in relation to the injury.
DRCA medical treatment compensation payable
    AND DRCA liability to pay compensation established
    AND medical treatment obtained

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 17 - Compensation in respect of medical treatment—practical arrangements
# Original: Medical treatment must be provided by an approved medical practitioner or approved hospital, except in emergencies or where specific Commission approval is granted.
DRCA medical treatment practical arrangements met
    AND DRCA medical treatment compensation payable
    AND provider pathway virtual ONE
        OR approved provider path
            AND medical provider status = "approved medical practitioner"
        OR approved hospital path
            AND medical provider status = "approved hospital"
        OR emergency provider path
            AND medical provider status = "emergency provider"

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 18 - Compensation in respect of medical treatment—amount
# Original: The amount of compensation payable for medical treatment is the amount that the relevant authority determines to be reasonable in the circumstances, often guided by scheduled medical fees.
DRCA medical treatment amount calculated
    AND DRCA medical treatment practical arrangements met
    AND medical costs are reasonable

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 19 - Compensation in respect of incapacity for work—preliminary
# Original: Establishes the preliminary definitions and framework for calculating incapacity payments, specifically defining Normal Weekly Earnings (NWE) and Actual Earnings (AE).
DRCA incapacity for work preliminary met
    AND DRCA liability to pay compensation established
    AND KNOWN normal weekly earnings
    AND KNOWN actual earnings

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 20 - Incapacity for work—employees who are not former employees
# Original: For current employees, the incapacity payment is calculated as the difference between their Normal Weekly Earnings (NWE) and their Actual Earnings (AE) while incapacitated.
DRCA incapacity for current employees calculated
    AND DRCA incapacity for work preliminary met
    AND incapacity status = "current employee"
    AND normal weekly earnings >= actual earnings

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 21 - Incapacity for work—former employees
# Original: For former employees, the compensation rate steps down after the first 45 weeks of incapacity. For the first 45 weeks, it is 100% of NWE minus AE; thereafter, it steps down to 75% of NWE minus AE.
DRCA incapacity step down percentage IS CALC (weeks of incapacity <= initial incapacity period weeks ? 100 : 75)
    NEEDS weeks of incapacity
    NEEDS initial incapacity period weeks

DRCA incapacity for former employees calculated
    AND DRCA incapacity for work preliminary met
    AND incapacity status = "former employee"
    AND KNOWN DRCA incapacity step down percentage
    AND normal weekly earnings >= actual earnings

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 22 - Incapacity for work—members of the Reserve forces
# Original: For Reservists, Normal Weekly Earnings (NWE) includes a specific reserve earnings component that combines their civilian employment income with their reserve service pay to ensure accurate compensation.
DRCA incapacity for reservists calculated
    AND DRCA incapacity for work preliminary met
    AND incapacity status = "reservist"
    AND KNOWN reserve earnings component

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 23 - Incapacity for work—other cases and superannuation offsets
# Original: Covers specific edge cases for incapacity and mandates that if a former employee receives a superannuation pension or lump sum, the incapacity payment must be reduced by a prescribed statutory adjustment amount to prevent dual compensation.
DRCA incapacity other cases and superannuation offsets applied
    AND DRCA incapacity for work preliminary met
    AND incapacity status = "other"
    AND superannuation pathway virtual ONE
        OR superannuation pension path
            AND superannuation pension received
            AND KNOWN superannuation adjustment amount
        OR superannuation lump sum path
            AND superannuation lump sum received
            AND KNOWN superannuation adjustment amount
        OR no superannuation path
            AND NOT superannuation pension received
            AND NOT superannuation lump sum received

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 24 - Compensation for permanent impairment
# Original: Where an injury results in a permanent impairment, the relevant authority is liable to pay compensation. However, no compensation is payable unless the degree of permanent impairment is at least 10%.
DRCA permanent impairment compensation payable
    AND DRCA liability to pay compensation established
    AND degree of permanent impairment assessed
    AND impairment is permanent
    AND impairment points >= permanent impairment minimum threshold percentage

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 25 - Interim compensation for permanent impairment
# Original: If the degree of permanent impairment cannot be finally determined but is assessed at 10% or more, an interim payment may be made based on the preliminary assessment.
DRCA interim permanent impairment compensation payable
    AND DRCA liability to pay compensation established
    AND interim impairment assessment required
    AND impairment points >= permanent impairment minimum threshold percentage
    AND NOT impairment is permanent

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 26 - Compensation for permanent impairment—former employees
# Original: Specific calculation rules apply to former employees to ensure their permanent impairment lump sums are correctly indexed and adjusted for any prior interim payments.
DRCA permanent impairment for former employees calculated
    AND DRCA permanent impairment compensation payable
    AND incapacity status = "former employee"

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 27 - Compensation for permanent impairment—members of the Reserve forces
# Original: Specific calculation rules apply to Reservists to account for their dual civilian and military earnings baselines when assessing non-economic loss.
DRCA permanent impairment for reservists calculated
    AND DRCA permanent impairment compensation payable
    AND incapacity status = "reservist"

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 28 - Compensation for permanent impairment—other cases
# Original: Covers the calculation of permanent impairment compensation for all other employee categories not explicitly defined as former employees or reservists.
DRCA permanent impairment other cases calculated
    AND DRCA permanent impairment compensation payable
    AND incapacity status = "other"

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 29 - Compensation in respect of death
# Original: Where an injury results in the death of the employee, the relevant authority is liable to pay compensation to the dependants of the deceased employee.
DRCA death compensation payable
    AND DRCA liability to pay compensation established
    AND death resulted from injury or disease

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 30 - Payments to dependants
# Original: The compensation for death is distributed among the dependants (wholly or partly dependent) according to their level of financial reliance on the deceased member.
DRCA payments to dependants calculated
    AND DRCA death compensation payable
    AND dependant status IS IN LIST: DVA dependant status
    AND NOT dependant status = "not dependent"

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 31 - Funeral expenses
# Original: The relevant authority is liable to pay a lump sum for funeral expenses incurred by the dependants or the estate of the deceased employee, up to the prescribed statutory maximum.
DRCA funeral expenses payable
    AND DRCA death compensation payable
    AND funeral expenses incurred
    AND funeral expenses amount > 0

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 32 - Exclusion of compensation where action brought against Commonwealth or licensed corporation
# Original: If an employee or dependant brings a common law action for damages against the Commonwealth or a licensed corporation in respect of an injury, statutory compensation under this Part is strictly excluded or suspended to prevent dual recovery.
DRCA exclusion where action brought applied
    AND common law action initiated against Commonwealth

# Reference: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025C00670/latest/text
# Section: Section 33 - Recovery of damages adjustments
# Original: Where an employee recovers damages from a third party in respect of an injury, the relevant authority may reduce, suspend, or recover the statutory compensation already paid to prevent double-dipping.
DRCA recovery of damages adjustments applied
    AND damages recovered from third party
    AND damages amount recovered > 0
    AND MANDATORY commission determination required
